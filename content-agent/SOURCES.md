## Restrictions / caveats
- Profile counts and caption snippets are not stable performance benchmarks.
- Public likes/comments do not reveal reach, saves, shares, watch time, completion or enquiries.
- Specialist roundups are editorial sources. They are useful for discovering authors and seeing curated examples but not objective proof that a particular technique performs better.
- A profile marked “watchlist” has not yet received a full review of multiple image sequences or Reels.
- The VECTA reference library is external research plus editorial rules. This is not model fine-tuning or a claim of changed underlying model weights.
- Authors’ photographs remain their copyrighted work; this library records analytical observations and links, not copied image files or instructions to imitate an identifiable image.
