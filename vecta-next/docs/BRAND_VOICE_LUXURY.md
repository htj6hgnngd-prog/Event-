# VECTA Weddings — Luxury brand direction (approved positioning)

## Audience and cultural frame

VECTA is positioned for **luxury wedding photo/video**, not the mass wedding market.
Primary audience: affluent couples prioritizing visual quality, design taste,
service clarity, confidence in execution, and wedding planners involved in
supplier choice. This is a **working target-audience hypothesis**, not a
validated audience research study.

The website must feel like a premium fashion/editorial visual brand, NOT a
bridal invitation card, conventional photography template, or SaaS homepage.

## Brand voice — hard restrictions

NO sentimental romantic marketing. Avoid phrases of this nature:
- "Навсегда", "Ваша любовь", "Одно слово. Целый мир", "Просто живите",
  "Не ставим любовь в позу", "Как вы хотите это помнить?"
- Teenage-poetry metaphors, emotional promises, or love slogans.
- Claims of awards, exclusive client list, exceptional teams, worldwide
  production, limited availability or booking without evidence.
- Fake testimonials, stock wedding photos presented as VECTA work.

DO:
- Short direct propositions grounded in the service.
- Use the customer's real selection vocabulary:
  "Портфолио", "Фото", "Видео", "Фото + видео",
  "Запросить предложение", "Дата", "Место проведения".
- Show results before making long claims.
- Explain composition, filming format, organization and conditions clearly.
- Bring qualified clients into a personal conversation, not a generic
  email/signup funnel.

## Visual rules

- Prefer black/ink, off-white, and disciplined burgundy-red signature.
- Brand-led high-contrast typography, controlled editorial asymmetry,
  exceptional spacing and plain backgrounds (NO gradients/vignettes).
- Preserve **Editorial Mosaic**: 1 large + 4 small cells; photo and short
  silent 2–4s video loop must fit IDENTICAL-sized cells.
- Place video loops within photographs, not in a segregated video carousel.
- The red element must be a brand signature, **not the word "Навсегда"**.
  Present candidate oversized V monogram; consider replacing it with more
  individually drawn graphic after art-direction review.
- No curtains, velvet, gold frames, standard SaaS tiles or clichés.
- The media placeholders remain empty until the owner gives genuine portfolio
  assets, even if this limits the perceived "wow" effect.

## Funnel

1. Hero: identify VECTA as luxury wedding photo/film in Moscow.
2. Portfolio is the first major section after the hero.
3. Approach: execution and art direction, not romantic poetry.
4. Three formats and detail links to the existing SEO pages.
5. Selecting a format takes the visitor to the enquiry panel with that format
   preselected and date field in focus.
6. Request form opens a Telegram draft link; it does NOT send a message or
   book a date. Never claim success without backend confirmation.
7. FAQ: questions about conditions, availability, planner coordination.
